# Barbarian Primitive costume

Approved concept direction: bare chest, leather harness, one fur-lined shoulder, hide skirt and hip panels. Review generated fit and animation.

Art only: no gameplay stats or equipment resources. Selected concept is approved; model outputs remain external review candidates until explicit publication.

Render with `studio.py armor compare costume_barbarian_primitive`. The body preset is `hero`.

The shared costume palette/gradient shading is used without overlay meshes for color. Hip plates and buckle follow hips; the royal chest/back follow chest, with the upper belly on spine. Shoulder caps use baked half-arm hinges around attached leather bearings. Fur is continuous closed geometry. Horn sockets, raised fittings and buckles are unioned with their supporting rigid parts.

Associated character previews: `studio.py armor character costume_barbarian_primitive --empty-hands`. This stages full dressed characters outside the repository and makes the torso visible without held weapons.
