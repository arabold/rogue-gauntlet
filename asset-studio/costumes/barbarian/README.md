# Barbarian costume

Existing blue barbarian torso, pants, harness, belt and cape preserved as one authored costume set.

Art only: no gameplay stats or equipment resources. Selected concept is approved; model outputs remain external review candidates until explicit publication.

Render with `studio.py armor compare costume_barbarian`. The body preset is `standard`.

The shared costume palette/gradient shading is used without overlay meshes for color. Hip plates and buckle follow hips; the royal chest/back follow chest, with the upper belly on spine. Shoulder caps use baked half-arm hinges around attached leather bearings. Fur is continuous closed geometry. Horn sockets, raised fittings and buckles are unioned with their supporting rigid parts.

Associated character previews: `studio.py armor character costume_barbarian --empty-hands`. This stages full dressed characters outside the repository and makes the torso visible without held weapons.

The caped version now uses a continuous smooth baldric instead of wrapping a polar chest strip into the narrowing neck. A single brown cape includes a smooth front chest bib and shoulder yoke continuing into its back drape; it has no separate cowl/gorget. The belt has a 0.135-high, 0.022-thick strap, a smaller round boss, a pronounced front widening and short, integrated fur linings along both edges. The original blue tunic, bear hood, cape and limb accessories remain.

The belt conforms to the actual tunic triangles at each height and angle. Extra height rows follow the torso taper instead of holding one maximum waist outline. The diagonal baldric is tucked beneath the band; its ends do not push the whole belt away from the tunic. A 0.135-high leather band retains the smaller silhouette while giving the buckle enough surrounding leather. The upper and lower fur borders are continuous pelt linings, embedded into their matching bevels and boolean-unioned with the band, so leather and fur share a closed exterior rather than disconnected triangular prisms. Root and hem points follow the same fitted tunic surface; hardware retains its original material and animation handling.
