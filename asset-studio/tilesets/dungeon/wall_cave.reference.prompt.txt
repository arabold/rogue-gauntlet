Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: concept reference for a cohesive low-poly fantasy game asset library.

Style/medium: KayKit-inspired friendly, chunky low-poly 3D game art, matching the supplied project character references. Every asset should belong beside those characters in the same game. Treat references as style guides only; do not include their characters, equipment, or layouts unless the subject requests them.

Shape language: bold readable silhouette, exaggerated proportions, substantial thickness, broad planar facets, softly chamfered edges and intentionally low polygon curves. Rounded forms still have visible large facets. Use a strong primary form plus a few distinctive secondary forms. Include recognizable functional details at a scale visible from an elevated gameplay camera: collars, rolled rims, sockets, handles, chain links, cut faces or holes when appropriate. Simplify detail into purposeful modeled shapes; do not reduce the design to a stack of generic primitives.

Character proportions and fidelity: when the subject is a character, match the supplied hero reference closely: an oversized head or helmet about one third of total height, a compact torso, very short thick limbs, simple mitten-like hands and chunky feet. Large creatures and armored bosses gain size through world scale and breadth while retaining this stylized vocabulary. Avoid realistic adult anatomy, long legs, small heads and detailed collectible-figurine armor. Resolve armor into a few substantial plates and a handful of readable fittings, with deliberately simple bevels and broad facets.

Color and shading: restrained harmonious palette, clean solid color regions and subtle broad light-to-dark ramps like the project's character palette shading. Matte surfaces by default; stylized metal, glass or gems only where requested by the subject. Soft neutral illumination with gentle form shading and restrained contact shading. Material identity comes from color, shape and broad highlights. Keep surface colors clean and readable.

Construction: depict a plausible model with real volume in all three dimensions. Parts meet at convincing attachment points; chains connect through loops, handles attach to their supports, and objects rest naturally. No unexplained floating details or disconnected decoration. Views of the same asset must agree geometrically.

Presentation: plain white background, full object visible with comfortable margins. No scenery, dramatic lighting, depth of field, text, labels, logos, borders or watermark. The task-specific layout below controls views and pose.

Avoid: photorealism, food photography, realistic surface scans, tiny pores, speckles, crumbs, scratches, photographic wood grain, gritty wear, painterly brushwork, outlines, glossy product photography, cinematic reflections, dense sculpted detail and smooth high-poly realism. Preserve useful medium-scale detail and character rather than adding surface noise.

Task-specific instructions:
Subject: a modular kit of cave and mine wall pieces for an elevated-camera dungeon crawler. They extend the existing cave wall shown in the last reference: big chunky faceted boulders in warm gray-brown stone heaped between squared timber posts on small stone footings, with an open rocky top and no header beams. Every piece uses the same grid: walls are 4 units long, 4 tall and 1 thick. Show six separate pieces: (1) a timber-framed mine doorway, two posts and a lintel beam around an open passage, rock heaped on either side; (2) a T-junction where three wall runs meet at one post; (3) a wall end where the rock heap tapers down into a low rubble slope; (4) a free-standing rock pillar bound by a timber collar; (5) a mine gate, posts with a few thick vertical timber bars; (6) a wall run with a diagonal wooden prop leaning against it. Layout: the six pieces in two rows on a plain white background, each in the same elevated three-quarter view as the game camera, consistent scale, generous spacing. Supplied images are style references only: the first for the overall art style, the second for the existing KayKit dungeon kit, the third for the cave wall these pieces must match.
