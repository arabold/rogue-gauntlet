# Shop rooms and future merchants

Status: not started. The [shop system](../shops.md) covers the playable Quartermaster: its realm placement, the trade tabs, prices, stock and saved purchases. This proposal covers what remains: a dedicated shop room, a deeper shelf, and later merchants.

## The Quartermaster's room

Today the keeper stands on a free tile near the arrival of each realm's first floor after the Garrison, and the shop is that floor's [headline](../level-design.md#current-generation). The realm's shop placement could ask for a shop room as that headline instead: an optional branch near the arrival with one unlocked entrance and a clear approach to the counter. A warm lamp, shelves of supplies and a sign should set it apart from ordinary storage at the gameplay camera distance. Generation keeps the current retry rule: a layout without room for the shop is re-rolled.

A visible ward at the threshold keeps monsters and combat effects outside and blocks attacks through the entrance in either direction. Pursuit ends at the threshold; enemies resume their normal behavior outside and keep their health and state. The ward covers ordinary pathing, forced movement, dashes, projectiles and area effects. Inside, mana regeneration and status timers run on their normal clocks. The room excludes generated monsters, nests, loot, chests, traps, fountains and progression pickups, and must keep an accessible route to the descent.

## A shelf for every depth

The Quartermaster's weapon and armor pools list only tier-1 pieces, so the shelves at depths 11, 16 and 21 carry no equipment, and its potions are always small. Give each stock pool entries for every tier (or a pool per realm) so the shelf follows the local tier band, and offer medium and large potions where they drop.

## Later merchants

An inn can sell drinks, food and travel supplies. An armory can specialize in local-tier weapons, armor and shields, including Magic and Rare equipment. Each reuses the merchant profiles for trade rules and brings its own keeper visuals and room dressing; greetings and farewells can give each location its own voice. Realms list their merchants on the realm, as they list the Quartermaster today; which realm gets which merchant is an open decision in the [realm content pacing plan](realm-content-pacing-plan.md).

Before adding merchants or stronger stock, playtest the Quartermaster with hurt and undergeared arrivals from each class: compare gold on arrival, what they buy and how they fare on the next realm's floors.
