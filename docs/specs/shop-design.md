# Shop rooms and future merchants

The [shop system](../shops.md) covers the playable Quartermaster: its realm placement, the trade tabs, prices, stock and saved purchases. This proposal covers what remains: a dedicated shop room and later merchants.

## The Quartermaster's room

Today the keeper stands on a free tile near the arrival. With per-depth room roles from the [progression plan](level-progression-prototype-plan.md), the realm's shop placement can ask for a shop room instead: an optional branch near the gatekeeper floor's arrival, before the Warden, with one unlocked entrance and a clear approach to the counter. A warm lamp, shelves of supplies and a sign should set it apart from ordinary storage at the gameplay camera distance. Generation keeps the current retry rule: a layout without room for the shop is re-rolled.

A visible ward at the threshold keeps monsters and combat effects outside and blocks attacks through the entrance in either direction. Pursuit ends at the threshold; enemies resume their normal behavior outside and keep their health and state. The ward covers ordinary pathing, forced movement, dashes, projectiles and area effects. Inside, mana regeneration and status timers run on their normal clocks. The room excludes generated monsters, nests, loot, chests, traps, fountains and progression pickups, and must keep an accessible route to the Warden and the descent.

## Later merchants

An inn can sell drinks, food and travel supplies. An armory can specialize in local-tier weapons, armor and shields, including Magic and Rare equipment. Each reuses the merchant profiles for trade rules and brings its own keeper visuals and room dressing; greetings and farewells can give each location its own voice. Later realms list their merchants on the realm, like the Garrison's Quartermaster.

Before adding merchants or stronger stock, playtest the Quartermaster with hurt and undergeared arrivals from each class: compare gold on arrival, what they buy and how they fare against the Warden.
