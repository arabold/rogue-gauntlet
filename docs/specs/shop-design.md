# Shop rooms and future merchants

The [shop system](../shops.md) covers the playable Quartermaster, trade screen, prices, stock and save data. This proposal covers dedicated shop rooms and expansions after the map redesign.

## The Quartermaster's room

Put the Quartermaster on an optional branch near the Depth 3 arrival, before the Warden. Give the room one unlocked entrance and a clear approach to the counter. A warm lamp, shelves of supplies and a sign should distinguish it from ordinary storage at the gameplay camera distance.

A visible ward at the threshold keeps monsters and combat effects outside and blocks attacks through the entrance in either direction. Pursuit ends at the threshold. Enemies resume their normal behavior outside and retain their health and state. The ward covers ordinary pathing, forced movement, dashes, projectiles and area effects. Inside, mana regeneration and status timers run on their normal clocks.

Use the authored room and threshold to exclude generated monsters, nests, loot, chests, traps, fountains and progression pickups. Verify that the placement fits and preserves an accessible route to the Warden and descent once the new generator is ready.

## Later merchants

An inn can sell drinks, food and travel supplies. An armory can specialize in local-tier weapons, armor and shields, including Magic and Rare equipment. Use the existing merchant profiles for trade rules and let the locations supply their own keeper visuals and room dressing. Greetings and farewells can give each location its own voice.

A future merchant could sell discounted cursed gear when the curse and its equip consequences are visible. Playtest the first shop with hurt and undergeared arrivals from each class. Compare gold on arrival, purchase choices and Warden outcomes before setting shop cadence and adding more powerful stock.
